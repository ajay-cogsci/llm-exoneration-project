Topic: forecasting from public environmental time series
Question: How does variation in missing-observation frequency affect imputation and short-horizon forecasting on public temperature sensor series?
Rationale: A low average forecast error may hide failure during extended outages; comparing missingness patterns can clarify when imputation ceases to be useful.
