Area: Computer vision
Topic: small-image classification under input changes
Question: How does variation in brightness variation affect the benefit and cost of training augmentation on public texture image collections?
Rationale: An augmentation can improve transformed-image accuracy while harming unchanged images, making aggregate accuracy an incomplete guide to robustness.
Contribution: Map where an augmentation improves transformed-image performance without unacceptable loss on unchanged inputs, including settings where the tradeoff reverses.
Method: Vary brightness variation under matched budgets and repeated training seeds, reserve transformation intensities for evaluation, and separate tuning images from the final test set.

Evaluation: Report paired accuracy differences and repeated-seed intervals for unchanged and held-out transformations; examine whether improvements persist beyond the tuning intensities.
